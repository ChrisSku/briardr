**Send several images or files at once, and drop files on the terminal.** "Select image…" and "Select file…" take more than one, and with a mouse or trackpad files can be dropped on the terminal. All of them are reviewed in one sheet (any can be removed), uploaded in order, and their paths inserted as one paste, never followed by Enter: for an agent each path on a line of its own, for a shell as arguments.
