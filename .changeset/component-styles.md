**The styles that Briardr's screens and built-in plugins keep in their own components now apply.** The client's build dropped them, so file icons in Files could shrink in a narrow row, a search inside files didn't lay out its results or the Names and Contents choice, the pairing QR code in Settings → Devices wasn't sized and centred, the quiet hours times in Settings → Notifications didn't sit side by side, and a new agent's prompt field didn't keep its line spacing. Installed plugins' own styles always applied.
