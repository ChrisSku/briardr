**Compose: write a message in a text field of your device, then insert it.** The key bar's + menu has "Compose…": a field with the device's autocorrection, editing and keyboard dictation (so dictation also works with a Linux host). Insert puts the message into the terminal as one paste, never followed by Enter. A message you don't insert is kept as the stream's draft, and the last messages you inserted are offered again under Recent; both stay on the device.
