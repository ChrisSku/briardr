**Choose the key bar's keys in Settings, with a row of their own for an app, and keep the on-screen keyboard up when a tap lands between keys.** Settings › Key bar picks the top row of the key bar from symbols, Control chords, Tab, Shift-Tab, Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, Forward Delete and F1–F12, up to 10 keys. That row is the default; give an app (Claude, Codex, a shell, Neovim, or any program running in a pane) keys of its own and the bar shows them instead while it runs. The choice is stored on each device. A tap in a gap between the bar's keys no longer takes focus from the terminal and closes the iOS keyboard, and while the keyboard is open the bar sits closer to it.
