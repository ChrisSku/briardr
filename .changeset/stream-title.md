**An agent's row shows what it's working on.** In the Agents and Workspaces lists and in Jump, an agent's row has a line with its terminal title (Claude Code sets it to the task at hand), unless the title only repeats the stream's name, the agent's own name or the folder. Jump and the Workspaces search find agents by it too. With the Herdr app, a stream named after its terminal's title no longer starts with the agent's spinner.
