**A stream can fill the screen: Full screen in its ⋯ menu (or ⇧⌘↩) hides the header and tabs, and a button in the top right corner brings them back.** It works in the terminal and every tool (Files, Changes, Preview, Docs, Notes and installed plugins); on iPad and Mac it also hides the sidebar and the list beside the stream, and ⌘1–⌘9 still switch tools. Leaving the stream ends full screen.
