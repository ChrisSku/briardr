**Each device chooses its own alerts, and can have quiet hours.** In Settings → Notifications, the switches for Needs you, Finished, Ready for input and low battery now apply to the device you set them on; before, turning one off on a phone turned it off for every device. A device that never chose follows the host's `notifications` setting, so nothing changes until you touch a switch. "Quiet hours" keeps a span of the device's day free of alerts (22:00 to 07:00 unless you change it). "Include names" stays one setting for every device, and is now labelled so.
